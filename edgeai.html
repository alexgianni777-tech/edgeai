How the edge is proven

Most "signals" are a backtest curve fitted to the past. EdgeAI is built to survive the opposite test.

1

Walk-forward, not curve-fit

Parameters are chosen on older data, then applied to a later window the model never saw. The numbers you see come only from that unseen data.

2

Out-of-sample only

Every stat — expectancy, win rate, profit factor — is measured on trades the strategy was never optimised on. No in-sample flattery.

3

Regime filter

Setups are only taken when the market index itself is above its 200-day average. Trading with the tide, not against it — and it measurably lifts the edge.

4

Open track record

Every signal is logged with a timestamp the moment it fires — win or lose. The record grows forward and can't be edited after the fact.

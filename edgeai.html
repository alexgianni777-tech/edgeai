How evidence is tested

EdgeAI separates candidate generation from evidence. A proposal can exist even when the historical evidence is weak; the label tells you which is which.

1

Market-level walk-forward

One common parameter set is selected across the market on older data, then applied unchanged to the next calendar-aligned unseen period.

2

OOS + uncertainty

Expectancy, hit rate and profit factor are measured on unseen trades, with a 95% expectancy interval and stability across walk-forward windows.

3

Point-in-time overlays

Long validation uses the index regime known on the signal bar, while RS is reconstructed cross-sectionally at that historical date. Shorts use their own weakness filter. Proposals are not silently hidden by these labels.

4

Forward-only proposal ledger

Displayed proposals are identified by ticker, setup and signal date, then resolved only from later bars. The ledger is not substituted for the strategy backtest.
